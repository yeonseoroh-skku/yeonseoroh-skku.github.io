About Me

Clothing & Textiles @ SKKU

Hello, I am Yeonseo Roh, a student majoring in Clothing & Textiles at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU). Welcome to my personal homepage!

I focus on exploring the intersection of fashion aesthetics and structural data analysis. By combining creative insight with functional web tools, I aim to transform trend paradigms into concrete, user-centric visual experiences.
